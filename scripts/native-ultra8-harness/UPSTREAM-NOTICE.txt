Grove Ultra8 helper upstream notice

The worker uses a modified parakeet-rs 0.3.7 source checkout at
1d6ffeae1b8641f497e4ef9a5e9fff37aa7a4181 (MIT OR Apache-2.0).
The patch increases the compile-time speaker output capacity from four to eight.
It does not train or alter model weights and does not enforce an exact speaker count.
The bundled worker uses ONNX Runtime 1.28.0 through ort 2.0.0-rc.13 (MIT OR Apache-2.0).
See the per-component licenses and ONNX Runtime third-party notices in this directory.

The helper's timestamp semantics follow the public default postprocessing parameters
in NVIDIA NeMo Speech at ea1ebf55b25fceff372f885b4e7b5803f3d33dea (Apache-2.0):
strictly greater than 0.5 starts, strictly less than 0.5 stops, equality retains
state; no median smoothing, padding, gap merging or minimum duration.
https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/blob/ea1ebf55b25fceff372f885b4e7b5803f3d33dea/nemo/collections/asr/parts/utils/vad_utils.py

Model weights are NOT included in the worker. The separately supplied Ultra8 ONNX
artifact comes from investguy/ultra_diar_streaming_sortformer_8spk_v1_onnx at revision
2a45f114eaf920b4d50b04a5964cc1aab35ddf5f, based on the author's
mago-ai/ultra_diar_streaming_sortformer_8spk_v1 fine-tune of NVIDIA Sortformer v2.1.
The author labels their weights/documentation Apache-2.0; the original NVIDIA base
has NVIDIA Open Model License terms. Those separate terms are not replaced by this
helper's source-code notices. Local testing is not a review of public redistribution.
